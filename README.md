# Zepto Dark Store Inventory Project

## Overview
This project simulates a realistic Data Warehouse and inventory management system for a quick-commerce network (like Zepto). It tracks the end-to-end lifecycle of products across multiple dark stores—from purchase orders and supplier deliveries to customer orders, daily stock movement, and calculated wastage (spoilage).

## Database Schema & Tables

The MySQL database (`zepto_inventory`) is divided into **Base Operational Tables** (simulating live transactional data) and **Analytical Fact Tables** (aggregated for BI and reporting).

### Base Operational Tables
These tables represent the raw data generated by the core WMS/ERP system.

1. **`products`**: The catalog of items (SKUs) sold across the network. Includes `mrp`, `unit_cost`, and `shelf_life_days`.
2. **`dark_stores`**: Details of the micro-fulfillment centers (dark stores), including their `capacity_units` and location.
3. **`suppliers`**: The vendors who supply the SKUs.
4. **`product_suppliers`**: A mapping table defining which supplier provides which SKU and at what `cost_price`.
5. **`customers`**: Details of the end-users ordering from the app.
6. **`orders`**: Header-level information for customer purchases (assigned to a specific `store_id` and `customer_id`).
7. **`order_items`**: Line-item details of what was sold in each order. Critically, this includes a `batch_id` to trace exactly which shipment the sold item came from.
8. **`purchase_orders`**: The inbound delivery logs. Each row represents a specific SKU arriving at a specific store.

### Analytical Fact Tables
These tables are generated via SQL scripts to optimize dashboards and reporting.

1. **`fact_daily_sales`** (`02_daily_items_sold.sql`):
   - Aggregates raw order items into a daily summary.
   - Shows the `total_quantity_sold` and `total_revenue` for each SKU, per store, per day.
   - Prevents the BI tool from running heavy 4-table joins on hundreds of thousands of rows.

2. **`fact_daily_movement`** (`03_daily_movement.sql`):
   - Combines inbound (purchases) and outbound (sales) into a single chronological ledger.
   - **Performance Optimization**: The Primary Key is `(movement_date, store_id, sku_id)` which forces InnoDB to use a *Clustered Index*. This guarantees the data is physically stored and queried in perfect chronological order by default.

3. **`fact_wastage_log`** (`04_wastage_log.sql`):
   - A reverse-engineered spoilage log. 
   - Compares the total amount of an item purchased in a specific batch against the exact amount of that batch that was sold.
   - Any unsold leftover quantity is marked as `quantity_wasted`.
   - The date of the waste is dynamically calculated as: `actual_delivery_date + shelf_life_days`.

## Key Business Logic & Modeling Decisions

### 1. The "Daily Wave" Batching Model (GRN)
In realistic fast-paced retail, batches often correspond to Goods Receipt Notes (GRNs) rather than individual manufacturer lot numbers. 
- In this dataset, deliveries are grouped by **Store** and **Delivery Date**. 
- If a dark store receives 50 different SKUs in their morning truck on December 12th, **all 50 SKUs share the exact same `batch_id`**. 
- This accurately models how a central warehouse dispatches daily consolidated waves to micro-fulfillment centers.

### 2. Day 0 "Initial Stock"
To ensure mathematical integrity (so that a store never sells more items than it physically possesses), an initial "Day 0" opening balance was generated.
- All unbatched historical sales were summed up and assigned a 5% buffer.
- This stock was injected into the system with a delivery date of `2024-12-12` (the very first day of operations).
- These opening balances were assigned the earliest `batch_id`s in the system (starting from `0`).

### 3. Wastage as a Calculation vs Event
In a live operational WMS, wastage is usually tracked as an explicit event (a worker scanning a barcode and flagging it as "Expired", or a nightly cron job moving expired stock to a waste ledger). 
For this analytical project, since we only have historical supply and demand data, wastage is **implicitly calculated** using a robust SQL CTE that matches sales against purchases and projects the expiration dates.

### 4. Stockout Rate Calculation (Denominator Logic)
The stockout rate denominator intentionally counts unique Store-SKU pairings from the sparse movement ledger and multiplies by 31 days. Because initial opening stock was injected on "Day 0" (December 12th), items that were stocked but never sold in January still appear in the ledger. This correctly rewards stores by including slow-moving but perfectly in-stock items in the total possible SKU-days, preventing artificial inflation of the stockout rate.

## KPI Views
The project includes several SQL scripts (in the `kpis` directory) to generate aggregate views for reporting and dashboards:

- **`view_abc_classification`** (`abc_classification.sql`): Categorizes SKUs into A, B, and C classes based on their sales and revenue contribution.
- **`view_inventory_turnover`** (`inventory_turnover_ratio_and_doh.sql`): Calculates the Inventory Turnover Ratio and Days of Inventory on Hand (DOH).
- **`view_network_summary`** (`network_summary.sql`): Provides a high-level network-wide overview. Metrics include total network revenue, total network profit, and the network-wide stockout rate percentage.
- **`view_sales_velocity`** (`sales_velocity.sql`): Calculates sales velocity and classifies items as Fast, Medium, or Slow moving.
- **`view_stock_out_incidents`** (`stock_out_incidents.sql`): Identifies specific days where stores completely ran out of specific SKUs.
- **`view_store_performances`** (`store_performances.sql`): Aggregates metrics at the store level, including total orders, delivered/cancelled orders, total revenue, gross profit, cancellation rate, average delivery time, on-time restock percentage, wastage, stockout rate, and the top performing category based on sales velocity.
- **`view_supplier_reliability`** (`supplier_reliability.sql`): Evaluates suppliers based on on-time delivery rates and lead times.
- **`view_wastage_info`** (`wastage_info.sql`): Aggregates total wastage quantities and monetary value per store and category.
